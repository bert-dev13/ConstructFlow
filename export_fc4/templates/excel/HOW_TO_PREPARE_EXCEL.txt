EXCEL TEMPLATE GUIDE — SWA & STEWA (Province of Cagayan)
=========================================================

Send your official .xlsx files. Place them here OR upload via the system:

  templates/excel/STEWA.xlsx
  templates/excel/SWA.xlsx

STEP 1 — PREPARE YOUR EXCEL FILE
--------------------------------
Open your official Word/Excel template in Microsoft Excel.

Wherever the system should insert data, type a placeholder in double curly braces:

  STEWA examples:
    {{project_name}}
    {{location}}
    {{contract_amount}}
    {{contractor}}
    {{period_covered}}
    {{contract_duration}}
    {{notice_to_proceed}}
    {{expiry_date}}
    {{percent_actual}}
    {{percent_planned}}
    {{slippage}}
    {{remarks}}
    {{report_date}}
    {{submitted_by_name}}
    {{noted_by_name}}

  SWA examples (header fields):
    {{project_name}}
    {{location}}
    {{report_date}}
    {{advance_payment}}
    {{total_contract_amount}}
    {{total_voucher}}

STEP 2 — SWA WORK ITEM TABLE (one template row)
-----------------------------------------------
In the accomplishment table, use ONE sample row with these placeholders:

  {{item_no}} | {{item_description}} | {{unit}} | {{unit_price}}
  {{programmed_qty}} | {{contract_amount}} | {{weight_pct}}
  {{previous}} | {{this_period}} | {{to_date}}
  {{accomplishment_weight_pct}} | {{remarks}}

The system finds the row containing {{item_no}} and duplicates it for each item.

STEP 3 — UPLOAD
---------------
Option A: Copy files to templates/excel/STEWA.xlsx and SWA.xlsx
Option B: Log in as Engineer IV → SWA/STEWA → Manage templates → Upload .xlsx

STEP 4 — GENERATE
-----------------
When a report is approved, the system:
  1. Opens your Excel template
  2. Fills all {{placeholders}}
  3. Expands SWA item rows
  4. Adds QR code on every sheet
  5. Saves filled .xlsx + PDF (if PDF export works; otherwise HTML PDF backup)

You can email your .xlsx files to the developer to map any special cells.
